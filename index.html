How to play

  1. Aim — drag left/right anywhere on the course. Tap the map button (or the mini-map) for a top-down view and tap where you want to go.
  2. Club — pick one from the bottom bar; the number is its full carry in yards.
  3. Swing — tap the big button to start, tap again to set power (the white mark shows what reaches the pin), then tap to strike.
  4. Strike zones — green = pure. Blue = draw (curves left), orange = fade (curves right). Miss them for a hook or slice.
  5. Spin — while the ball is in the air, drag: up = backspin (stops quick), down = topspin (runs out), sideways bends it.
  6. Greens — flowing arrows run downhill; red is steep. With the putter, the dotted line shows how the putt will break at pin distance.
  7. Lies — rough, sand and driver off the deck cost power and control. Water is a one-stroke penalty.
